Ask the brain — UI directions

Four ways to replace the right side-panel, each with its own ⌘K open micro-animation. Press Open (or click the top pill) in each to feel the motion. All on the current dark + lavender brand. Sample answer cites brain nodes as chips.

A · Spotlight command palette

recommended

Centered, focused, keyboard-native. ⌘K lifts a Raycast-style palette into the middle of the screen over a blurred app. Feels like "summoning" the brain. Animation: the top pill presses, then the palette scales up from 0.97 + fades, scrim blur eases in (spring curve).

B · Bottom bar that grows up

conversational

Composer pinned to the bottom; answers stack upward. Like a desktop assistant — you stay anchored on the input. Animation: the bar slides up from the bottom edge and the messages cascade in above it (staggered fade-rise). Great for quick back-and-forth without leaving the page.
