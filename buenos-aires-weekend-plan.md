# Weekend in Buenos Aires — Plan

> Note: `plan.md` in this directory is an existing software project plan (`tui-pilot`)
> and was deliberately left untouched. This travel plan lives in its own file.

## Assumptions (chosen defaults, since unspecified)
- **Travelers:** 2 adults, no mobility constraints, comfortable walking 15k+ steps/day.
- **Trip length:** Friday evening arrival → Sunday night (2 full days + 2 partial).
- **Season/weather:** Season-agnostic; swap outdoor blocks for indoor on rain days.
- **Budget:** Mid-range (comfortable restaurants, taxis/ride-share, paid attractions).
- **Base neighborhood:** Palermo (Soho/Hollywood) — central, safe, walkable, good food/nightlife.
- **Language:** Basic Spanish helpful; English common in tourist areas.
- **Payment:** Card widely accepted; carry some cash (pesos) for taxis, markets, tips.
- **Local rhythm:** Dinner starts late (21:00+); nightlife after midnight.

## Verification model
Each step lists a **Verify** check. "Verified" = booking confirmation in hand, reservation
reference saved, or item physically packed/installed on phone before departure.

---

## Phase 1 — Pre-trip logistics (do first, in order)

1. **Confirm dates & flights.** Book round-trip into EZE (Ezeiza, intl) or AEP (Aeroparque, regional).
   - *Verify:* flight confirmation email with PNR saved offline.
2. **Check entry requirements.** Passport valid 6+ months; confirm visa/waiver and any entry fees for your nationality.
   - *Verify:* official government source checked; screenshot saved.
3. **Book accommodation in Palermo.** 2 nights (Fri, Sat), free-cancellation rate, near Plaza Serrano.
   - *Verify:* booking confirmation with address + check-in instructions saved offline.
4. **Arrange airport transfers.** Pre-book remís/transfer or plan official taxi (EZE ~45–60 min, AEP ~20 min); note Cabify/Uber availability.
   - *Verify:* transfer voucher saved, or ride-share app installed with payment added.
5. **Money & connectivity.** Notify bank; get eSIM/local SIM or confirm roaming; download offline Google Maps for BA.
   - *Verify:* eSIM/SIM active; offline map downloaded; one card tested for international use.
6. **Reserve key dinners & a show (~1–2 weeks ahead).** Sat-night tango show/milonga; one notable parrilla (steakhouse).
   - *Verify:* reservation references saved with times in calendar.
7. **Travel insurance + offline document pack.** Passport scan, bookings, addresses, emergency numbers.
   - *Verify:* policy number saved; offline documents folder populated.

---

## Phase 2 — Friday (arrival + easy evening)

8. **Arrive, transfer, check in, settle.** Drop bags, freshen up.
   - *Verify:* checked in; phone on local network.
9. **Low-key Palermo evening.** Walk Plaza Serrano; dinner at a Palermo parrilla or bistro; optional cocktail bar.
   - *Verify:* dinner honored; early-ish night to recover from travel.

---

## Phase 3 — Saturday (city core: Centro, Recoleta, San Telmo, La Boca)

10. **Morning — Centro/Microcentro.** Plaza de Mayo (Casa Rosada, Cabildo, Catedral), walk Av. de Mayo.
    - *Verify:* arrived at Plaza de Mayo.
11. **Late morning — Recoleta.** Recoleta Cemetery (Evita's tomb), Floralis Genérica.
    - *Verify:* cemetery visited.
12. **Lunch — Recoleta/Retiro café.**
    - *Verify:* seated, fed.
13. **Afternoon — San Telmo.** Cobblestone streets, Mercado de San Telmo, antique shops.
    - *Verify:* reached Mercado de San Telmo.
14. **Late afternoon — La Boca / Caminito** (daylight only; stay on tourist streets; taxi in/out).
    - *Verify:* Caminito visited and departed before dusk.
15. **Evening — Tango show / milonga** (pre-booked Step 6), dinner around it.
    - *Verify:* reservation honored.

> **Note:** The famous **San Telmo Sunday Feria** runs Sundays on Defensa St. If you'd rather
> hit that market, move San Telmo browsing to Sunday and use Saturday afternoon for Puerto
> Madero + Reserva Ecológica instead.

---

## Phase 4 — Sunday (markets, parks, relaxed close)

16. **Morning — San Telmo Sunday Feria** (Defensa St.) **or** Mataderos Feria (gaucho culture, farther out).
    - *Verify:* arrived at chosen market.
17. **Midday — Palermo parks.** Bosques de Palermo, Rosedal, optional Japanese Garden or MALBA.
    - *Verify:* at least one park/museum visited.
18. **Lunch — Palermo brunch/parrilla.**
    - *Verify:* seated, fed.
19. **Afternoon — flex buffer + souvenirs.** Leather goods, alfajores, wine; repack.
    - *Verify:* bags packed; souvenirs bought.
20. **Departure transfer** with airport buffer (3h intl / 2h regional before flight).
    - *Verify:* at airport with margin; boarding pass ready.

---

## Phase 5 — Contingencies & references

21. **Rain plan:** MALBA, Bellas Artes, Teatro Colón tour, Galerías Pacífico, café-hopping.
22. **Safety:** Ride-share/radio taxis at night; keep valuables discreet; La Boca daytime only.
23. **Food hit-list:** parrilla/asado, empanadas, choripán, milanesa, alfajores, Malbec, dulce de leche, helado.
24. **Reference:** keep this plan + all confirmations in one offline folder accessible without data.
